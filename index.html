ABOUT / 002

Built for operators who are tired of chasing leads by hand

I'm a CRM and automation specialist working with clients through Fiverr and Upwork. My job is simple to describe and hard to get right: take the scattered parts of a business's sales process — the forms, the spreadsheets, the DMs, the sticky notes — and turn them into one CRM system with automation doing the repetitive work in the background.

That usually means setting up or restructuring a CRM, mapping out the pipeline stages that match how the business actually sells, and wiring up the automations — follow-up sequences, lead routing, reminders, reporting — so deals stop falling through the cracks.
